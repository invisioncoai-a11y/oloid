# OLOID Content Audit & Reference Website Analysis

## 1. Executive Summary

This document presents a comprehensive content audit of the reference website [https://www.taqamideast.com/](https://www.taqamideast.com/) conducted for the design and development of the new **OLOID** corporate website ([https://oloid.company](https://oloid.company)).

The reference website belongs to *TAQA Industrial and Petroleum Services Ltd*, an industrial equipment and technical services provider operating across Iraq and the Middle East, with historical affiliation to Saudi Arabian Development Company Ltd (SADC).

The new OLOID website retains **100% content coverage** of all business divisions, technical solutions, equipment categories, partner relationships, and regional footprint, while:
1. Rebranding the corporate presentation exclusively to **OLOID**.
2. Elevating the visual identity from a legacy 2011-era static layout to a world-class, premium corporate industrial standard inspired by the official OLOID logo.
3. Structuring content into modern, modular, API-ready data schemas.
4. Flagging all legacy legal entity claims, historical dates, and contact details for business review.

## 3. Product & Service Breakdown (Verified)

### Category 1: Mechanical & Rotating Equipment
- **Valves:** High-pressure ball, gate, globe, check, and control valves.
- **Actuators and Smart Positioners:** Pneumatic, electric, and hydraulic actuation systems.
- **Pumps:** Centrifugal, positive displacement, booster, and multiphase pumping units.
- **Pipe and Fittings:** Carbon steel, stainless steel, and alloy high-pressure piping components.
- **Flow Elements:** Orifice plates, venturi tubes, flow nozzles, and pitot tubes.
- **Turbines and Compressors:** Industrial steam/gas turbines, reciprocating, and rotary screw compressors.
- **Process Equipment:**
  - Shell and tube heat exchangers, air-cooled exchangers
  - Industrial cooling towers
  - Waste and environmental effluent treatment packages
- **Dryers & Filtration Systems:** Desiccant dryers, membrane dryers, coalescing particulate filters.

### Category 2: Electrical, Control & Instrumentation Equipment
- **SCADA & Leak Detection:** Real-time pipeline monitoring, flow modeling, acoustic and pressure wave leak detection.
- **Human Machine Interface (HMI):** Specialized software applications, DCS interfaces, industrial touch panels.
- **Instruments (Sensors & Gauges):** Pressure, temperature, level, and density transmitters.
- **Flow Measurement & Analyzers:** Fiscal metering, ultrasonic flow meters, gas chromatographs, moisture analyzers.
- **Metering & Loading Systems:** Custody transfer metering skids, truck/rail loading racks.
- **Electrical Motors & Drives:** Low and medium voltage induction motors, variable frequency drives (VFDs).
- **Environmental Monitoring:** Ambient air quality monitoring, continuous emission monitoring systems (CEMS).
- **Programmable Logic Controllers (PLCs):** Redundant safety instrumented systems (SIS) and fail-safe automation.
- **Industrial Communications & Networking:** Industrial Ethernet, fiber-optic telemetry, wireless field networks.
- **Security & Public Address (PA/GA):** Plant-wide acoustic warning, integrated CCTV, explosion-proof PA systems.
- **Fire & Gas Systems (FGS):** Flame detectors, toxic/combustible gas sensors, deluge control panels.

### Category 3: Upstream, Drilling & Completion Products
- **Electric Submersible Pumps (ESP):** High-temperature, high-volume artificial lift pumps.
- **Transfer Pumps:** Surface booster and crude oil transport units.
- **Gas Lift Mandrels & Valves:** Side-pocket mandrels, injection and orifice valves.
- **Wellheads, Chokes & Christmas Trees (C-Trees):** Surface wellhead equipment rated up to 15,000 PSI, adjustable and positive chokes.
- **Early Production Systems (EPS):** Mobile test separators, skidded degassing stations, heater treaters.
- **Well Production Monitoring & Optimization:** Downhole quartz gauges, multi-phase flow metering.
- **Downhole Tools & Replacement Parts:** Fishing tools, packers, tubing anchors, elastomeric seals.

### Category 4: Specialized Industrial Engineering Services
- **Small-to-Medium Scale EPC Construction:** Skidded plant modules, structural platforms, piping tie-ins.
- **Electro-Mechanical Works:** Heavy rotating machinery overhaul, motor rewinding, dynamic balancing.
- **Systems Integration & Commissioning:** Automation panel engineering, PLC/SCADA programming, factory acceptance testing (FAT), site acceptance testing (SAT).
- **Installation & Calibration:** Custody transfer skid calibration, loop checking, field sensor certification.
- **Valve & Actuator Maintenance:** In-situ testing, lapping, hydrostatic testing, overhaul and re-certification.
- **Waste & Environmental Consultancy:** Industrial wastewater treatment design, hazardous effluent management.
- **Data Management & Optimization:** Plant telemetry telemetry, asset health dashboards, predictive maintenance.
- **Technical Manpower Supply:** Certified instrumentation engineers, rotating equipment specialists, commissioning technicians.
