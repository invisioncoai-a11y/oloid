Welcome to Taqa Industrial and Petroleum Services Ltd

 

TAQA has a goal of modernizing and developing the major industrial sectors of energy, Oil & Gas, petrochemicals, mechanical & rotating equipment, utilities and other manufacturing in Iraq. TAQA is keen to work with leading global engineering, technology and services companies in order to successfully achieve this goal. To meet the growing needs of the local and regional market, we build and develop long-term and value added relationships with leading international industrial technology and engineering services partners. We are committed to our customers and partners with trust and quality as our utmost target.

TAQA is a sister company of Saudi Arabian Development Company Ltd (SADC) which is operating in Saudi Arabia, the Gulf area and North Africa for over 30 years. SADC is specialized in serving the petroleum and energy sectors and has subsidiaries and investments in other companies such as Technip Saudi Arabia, Hamon D'Hondt Middle East Ltd, etc.
