Our Mission

Taqa Industrial and Petroleum Services Ltd is a company with a mission to make a difference on Iraq's major industrial sector including petroleum, power, water, steel and other large manufacturing operations by modernizing it. TAQA aims to introduce the best of breed oil & gas, and industrial technology, services and know-how from our global partners. TAQA aims to establish strong relations with its partners that aim to lead to long term cooperation such as establishing joint ventures.

TAQA has two main divisions; Mechanical & Instrumentation and Controls. Each division has its own sales force and technical staff to ensure that all customers’ needs are met and especially after-sales support.

TAQA has a vision to be Iraq’s premiere industrial and petroleum technologies provider and to help modernize Iraq’s industrial sector to bring it to international standards.
